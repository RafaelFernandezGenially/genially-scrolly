# Route: story

For brand stories, explainers, histories, editorial pieces.

**Skeleton (10–14 beats):** opener (reveal or particles) → the big idea (words) → how it works (steps, 3–5 steps) → a turn (kinetic or zoom-through) → evidence (chart or kpi) → the map of it (camera) → history (timeline or horizontal) → a breather (marquee or color-flow) → close with one call to action (reveal + magnetic).

**Blocks that fit:** reveal, words, scramble, marker, steps, kinetic, zoom-through, portal, camera, timeline, horizontal, chart, kpi, compare, marquee, color-flow, magnetic.

**Traps:** a story is not a catalogue of effects: every block must carry a message from the storyboard. Keep the chapter labels (`data-chapter`) short (≤ 18 characters). Put the strongest visual in the first two screens.
